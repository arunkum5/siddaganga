About the trust

Service before self

Sri Siddaganga Charitable Trust is guided by the vachana philosophy of Basavanna — a call for equality, honest labour, and selfless service to all, regardless of caste or standing. This is the same spirit that has long defined the Siddaganga tradition: quiet, disciplined seva carried out without expectation of return.

The trust works to extend that tradition through education for children who need it most, dasoha (food seva) for those who go without, and support for the wider community in times of need.

"Kayakave Kailasa" — work itself is the path to the divine.

A central teaching of Basavanna, carried forward in the trust's daily work.
Our seva

Where the trust's work is focused

Four areas of ongoing service, each carried out quietly and consistently.

Education

Supporting schooling and hostel care for children from families who could not otherwise afford it.

Dasoha — food seva

Free meals offered without distinction, in keeping with the long-standing dasoha tradition.

Healthcare support

Assistance with basic medical needs and health camps for underserved families nearby.

Community welfare

Broader support for the local community — relief, guidance, and everyday assistance where needed.
